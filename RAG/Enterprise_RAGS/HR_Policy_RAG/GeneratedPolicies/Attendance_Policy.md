# Employee Punctuality and Attendance Policy

## 1. Introduction

Acme Corp values the dedication and commitment of its employees. Punctual and consistent attendance is crucial for maintaining operational efficiency, team collaboration, and delivering exceptional service to our clients in the Information Technology sector. This policy outlines the expectations for employee attendance and the procedures for managing absences and tardiness.

This policy does not supersede or replace provisions related to legally protected leaves such as those under the Maternity Benefit Act, Employees' State Insurance Act, or other applicable Indian labor laws, nor does it cover absences approved as reasonable accommodations under relevant disability legislation.

## 2. Purpose

The objective of this policy is to establish clear guidelines and consistent practices for managing employee attendance at Acme Corp. By promoting regular attendance and addressing instances of tardiness or absence effectively, we aim to enhance productivity, minimize disruptions, and foster a reliable work environment.

## 3. Attendance Standards and Reporting Procedures

Employees are expected to:

*   Arrive at work on time for their scheduled shifts.
*   Be prepared to commence work at the start of their shift.
*   Complete their full scheduled shift unless an approved absence or early departure is granted.
*   Notify their immediate supervisor or designated HR representative as soon as possible, and no later than [Specify Time, e.g., one hour] before the scheduled start of their shift, in the event of an unavoidable absence.
*   Provide a clear reason for the absence and an estimated return date.
*   Follow the designated procedure for reporting absences, which can be found in the [Link to or description of the absence reporting process, e.g., HR portal, specific email address].

## 4. Absence and Tardiness Management

Acme Corp utilizes a system to track attendance infractions. The following points are assigned:

*   **Late Arrival (Tardy):** 0.5 points
*   **Early Departure:** 0.5 points
*   **Unapproved Absence (No Call, No Show):** 2 points
*   **Approved Absence (with prior notification and approval):** 0 points
*   **Late Return from Lunch Break (exceeding 15 minutes without prior approval):** 1 point

A grace period of [Specify Time, e.g., 5 minutes] is permitted at the beginning and end of each scheduled workday, and for scheduled breaks.

Attendance infractions are tracked over a rolling [Specify Period, e.g., 12-month] period.

## 5. Disciplinary Actions for Attendance Infractions

Consistent adherence to attendance standards is expected. Failure to meet these standards may result in progressive disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. The following outlines the typical disciplinary steps:

*   **Accumulation of 3 points:** Verbal Counseling and documented discussion with supervisor.
*   **Accumulation of 4 points:** Formal Written Warning.
*   **Accumulation of 5 points:** Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) and potential suspension without pay.
*   **Accumulation of 6 points:** Termination of Employment.

**Important Considerations:**

*   **Extended Absences:** For absences exceeding three (3) consecutive working days, employees are required to submit a medical certificate or other appropriate documentation to the HR department.
*   **Job Abandonment:** An employee who is absent for three (3) consecutive working days without notifying their supervisor or HR will be considered to have voluntarily resigned from their position (job abandonment).
*   **Exclusions:** Absences for approved leave under applicable Indian labor laws (e.g., maternity leave, sick leave with medical certification), jury duty, or military service, when properly documented and approved, will not be counted as attendance infractions.

## 6. Unpaid Leave and Special Circumstances

Acme Corp recognizes that unforeseen circumstances may necessitate employee absence. In such cases, employees may request unpaid leave. Requests for unpaid leave should be submitted in writing to their supervisor and the HR department for review and approval on a case-by-case basis. Documentation may be required to support the request.

## 7. Inability to Clock In/Out

Employees are required to accurately record their work time by clocking in at the start of their shift and clocking out at the end of their shift. If an employee experiences an issue with the timekeeping system, they must immediately report it to their supervisor or the HR department to ensure accurate record-keeping. Failure to properly record work time may lead to disciplinary action.

## 8. Exceptions and Approved Absences

The following types of absences are generally excluded from disciplinary action, provided proper notification and documentation are submitted within [Specify Timeframe, e.g., 48 hours] of the absence:

*   Approved leave under applicable Indian labor laws.
*   Jury Duty.
*   Military Service.
*   Bereavement Leave (as per company policy).

Acme Corp reserves the right to review and approve or deny any exceptions to this policy based on individual circumstances and business needs.
